# Ledger City

A browser economy game about working, trading and building a fortune in a city that runs on its own economy. Inspired by Torn.

**Play:** https://ledgercity.co.uk

Ledger City is in beta. It is not public yet, it is updated often, and things may change or break. Feedback is welcome, and the form is linked from the game's title screen and menu.

## What you can do

- **Work.** Farming, foraging, mining and more. Every shift raises that job's skill, which makes your finds come in bigger stacks.
- **Learn.** Education courses unlock better jobs. Reading books at the Public Library raises your Intelligence, which makes every course finish faster.
- **Trade.** A market where prices follow supply and demand. Selling a lot pushes a price down, and a flood of sales from others keeps it down. Events and city orders shift it further.
- **Pick a path.** Gatherer is the default. Criminal and Enforcer paths bring different loot, and players sell that loot to each other to get what they need.
- **Fight.** Train at the gym, equip armour, and take on fights in the Brawl zone.
- **Look after yourself.** Run out of life or overdose and you end up in hospital. Medkits and morphine from the pharmacy cut your stay.
- **Build.** Rent better homes, join gyms, collect achievements and give to the Ledger Foundation.
- **Reach the end.** Hit level 25 with $1M to see the ending.

## Saving

Your progress is saved in your browser on the address you play on. It is not stored on a server, so clearing site data or switching browser or device starts a fresh game. Use **Save backup** in the game to keep a copy of your save, and **Load backup** to restore it. Cloud saves with accounts are planned.

## Running it yourself

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. Open it in a browser. There is no build step and nothing to install. The repository is served with GitHub Pages.
